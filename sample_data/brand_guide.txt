StrideWorks Running Shoes — Brand Knowledge Base

Company Overview:
StrideWorks is a performance running shoe company founded in 2019, based in Portland, Oregon.
We specialize in lightweight, carbon-plated running shoes for competitive and recreational runners.

Target Audience:
Our primary audience is runners aged 25-45 who run at least 3 times per week, training for
5Ks, half marathons, or full marathons. Secondary audience includes CrossFit and gym enthusiasts
who want a versatile daily trainer.

Brand Voice:
StrideWorks communicates with energy and authenticity. We avoid corporate jargon. Our tone is
motivational but never preachy — we speak like a running coach who's also your friend. We use
short, punchy sentences in social content and slightly more technical language in product pages.

Key Products:
- StrideWorks Velocity Pro: carbon-plated racing shoe, 220g, designed for marathon PRs.
- StrideWorks Trail Grip: trail running shoe with aggressive tread for off-road terrain.
- StrideWorks Daily Trainer: cushioned everyday shoe for recovery runs and daily mileage.

Past Campaign Performance:
Our "Run Your Story" Instagram campaign (2024) achieved a 4.2% engagement rate, well above
the industry average of 1.5%. User-generated content featuring real customer race stories
outperformed studio product photography by 3x in engagement.

Marketing Goals for Next Quarter:
Increase brand awareness among first-time marathon runners. Grow email list by 20%.
Launch the Velocity Pro on TikTok targeting Gen Z runners, a currently underdeveloped segment.

SEO Priorities:
Target keywords: "carbon plate running shoes", "marathon training shoes", "lightweight racing shoes".
Competitors ranking above us: Brand X, Brand Y. We should emphasize the 220g weight advantage.
